Farm Fresh Groceries

Farm Fresh Foods was designed to solve a deceptively simple problem: people want fresh, local food but don't know where to find it.

Created during my courses, the app connects users directly to local farmers and markets using GPS-based discovery, with profiles that tell the story behind the food and not just the price.

Features include:

  • Planned meal tools
  • Healthy leftover ideas
  • And a surplus donation system to feed those in need.

Designed with community at its core, and all from sketch to medium-fidelity working prototype in Figma.
