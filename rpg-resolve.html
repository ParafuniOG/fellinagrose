The RPG Resolve

Some of the best ideas start as a joke.

Mine started when I overheard a group of athletic friends comparing their running achievements. As a self-described dork — more at home behind a keyboard or a character sheet than a finish line — I laughed and said the only way I would ever run was if there was a Dragon to slay at the end of it.

That joke became a journey.

I started a journal and exercised the way I would if I was a character in a tabletop RPG. Cardio became Rogue quests. Resisting the office donuts became Detect Traps. All of which I earned XP from. Making smoothies leveled up my Alchemics. Rest days weren't failures. They were time spent at the Tavern, building up Rested XP for the next quest. I didn't think anyone else would ever want to do the same thing.

RPG Resolve is a fitness and wellness app that replaces guilt-based metrics with a high-fantasy narrative. It is built on the belief that rest is strategy, not failure; that every pace is valid, and that the right community makes every journey possible.

There are five classes:

  • the Warrior
  • the Rogue
  • the Cleric
  • the Oracle
  • and the Mage

Each one representing a different dimension of real human health, and each one built from lived experience rather than theory.

This is not an app I designed for a market. It is a system I built to survive, translated into something others can hold and use.

I have been the user for over a decade. Now I am building the door.
