ParaPhernalia

Real places. Real sensors. Unreal things.

A mobile investigation game where your phone's own sensors become the kit — and the folklore you were warned about hunts the world just outside your door.

You are a Digital Detective. Probably.

The Case

Your neighborhood was always haunted.
Now you can prove it.

ParaPhernalia turns the ordinary hardware in your pocket — the camera, the compass, the accelerometer, the microphone — into an investigator's toolkit. You don't chase invented monsters across a fictional map. You investigate where you actually are, and the things you find are drawn from the world's own folklore: the ones with names, the ones with none, and the ones your grandmother told you never to answer.

How you investigate matters. The careful detective gathers evidence. The careless one becomes it. That's all I'll say here — the rest is still in the dark where it belongs.

Who You Bring

Four ways to be wrong about a ghost.

ParaPhernalia is built for a party. Every role sees a different slice of the truth, and none of them is the whole picture — which is exactly why you need each other.

The Skeptic

Trusts the readings.

Doubts everything the readings can't explain. Keeps the case honest.

The Believer

Sees the pattern.

Finds the shape of it before there's proof. Usually right. Occasionally too soon.

The Sensitive

Feels what sensors can't.

Reads the room the instruments miss. Pays for it later.

The Magnet

Trouble finds them first.

Whatever's in the house, it wants them. Handy. Not restful.
